# CV-003.DS-001 - Three-Altitude Journey Workspace Foundation

**Status:** 🟠 In Progress

## Outcome

Navigator can move among Operational, Tactical and Strategic as durable workspace altitudes over one selected Journey while the existing conversation remains fully usable and runtime ownership remains unchanged.

## Promotion Decision

The Navigator accepted the three-altitude spatial grammar as product direction. This delivery story no longer asks whether the experiment should be discarded. It establishes the permanent workspace shell that subsequent delivery stories hydrate with real operational artifacts, tactical derivation, strategic meaning and synthesis checkpoints.

Representative data remains explicitly non-authoritative wherever a real data capability has not yet been delivered. Promotion commits the interaction and spatial model, not the fixture content or an unproven semantic model.

## Foundation Slice

- Preserve the current Journey selection sidebar as the stable navigation axis.
- Keep Operational, Tactical and Strategic as persistent changes of altitude over the same Journey.
- Keep the existing conversation functional inside Operational.
- Alternate Conversation and Artifacts at full workspace width.
- Establish a durable Tactical shell for missions, evidence and deliverables.
- Establish a durable Strategic shell for realizations, impacts and pragmatic/integrative value.
- Use visibly provisional representative content until DS-002, DS-003 and DS-004 hydrate each shell.
- Give each altitude a distinct density and rhythm while preserving the shared application shell.

## Validation Intent

Run the real desktop app and evaluate whether changing altitude feels like moving closer to or farther from the same Journey. Validate the promoted shell's continuity, accessibility and operational safety before downstream stories replace representative content with real projections and derivations.

## Done Condition

This foundation is done when all three durable workspace shells are navigable in the Harness; Operational conversation remains usable; Conversation and Artifacts retain full workspace width; provisional content is visibly non-authoritative; Journey selection, conversation continuity and runtime safety contracts remain intact; automated characterization is green; and the Navigator has accepted the aggregate foundation or recorded bounded corrections.

## Boundary

- This delivery story commits the product's spatial and navigation grammar, not the final Nautilus information model.
- Real Journey documentation projection remains in CV-003.DS-002.
- Real tactical and strategic derivation remain in CV-003.DS-003 and CV-003.DS-004.
- Provenance, confidence, correction and synthesis checkpoints remain in CV-003.DS-005.
- No altitude or area navigation may implicitly invoke Pi, Mirror or a provider.
- Representative data cannot be treated as live filesystem or derived Journey truth.
- Existing accepted child evidence remains historical evidence of the experiment that produced this foundation.
