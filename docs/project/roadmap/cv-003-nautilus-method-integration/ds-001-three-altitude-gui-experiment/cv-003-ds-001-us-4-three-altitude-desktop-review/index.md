# CV-003.DS-001.US-4 — Three-Altitude Foundation Review

**Status:** 🟡 Planned
**Type:** User Story

## Story Artifacts

- [Plan](plan.md)
- [Test Guide](test-guide.md)

## User Story

As the Navigator,
I want to experience the complete promoted foundation in the real desktop Harness,
so that I can accept its aggregate continuity or record bounded corrections before real semantic hydration continues.

## Outcome

The complete three-altitude foundation is reviewed in Tauri and receives explicit acceptance or a bounded correction list. Discarding the spatial grammar is no longer the default decision under review because the Navigator has already promoted it as product direction.

## Acceptance Behavior

```text
Given the promoted workspace foundation and a real selected Journey
When I traverse Operational, Tactical and Strategic
Then their distinct rhythm and shared-territory continuity remain coherent
And returning to Operational preserves the working cockpit.
```

```text
Given Journey-contextual representative content in a derived workspace shell
When I inspect that altitude and switch Journeys
Then the permanent composition remains distinguishable from downstream live derivation
And no fixture crosses its owning Journey boundary or claims live truth.
```

## Scope

- Real desktop aggregate review of the promoted workspace foundation.
- Journey switching and Operational continuity checks.
- Accessibility, spatial rhythm and full-width workspace checks.
- Explicit foundation acceptance or bounded correction record.
- Aggregate automated baseline.
- Clear handoff to CV-003.DS-003 through CV-003.DS-005.

## Out Of Scope

- Tactical or Strategic live derivation.
- Reopening accepted direction without contradictory evidence.
- Unbounded visual redesign during aggregate review.
- Treating representative content as production data.

## Validation

Follow the story test guide, validate the foundation in the real desktop app, and record Navigator acceptance or a bounded correction list.
