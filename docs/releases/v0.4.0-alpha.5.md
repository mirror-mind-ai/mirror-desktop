# v0.4.0-alpha.5 - Only the Conversations Made Here

**Date:** 2026-11-06

## Highlights

- **Only Nautilus's own conversations.** Conversations the Mirror Core created elsewhere (the
  terminal, Pi, Claude Code) are no longer listed, opened, continued or renamed in the app, and the
  "Desktop only" button is gone (CR206).
- **New creates where you pressed it.** New under a Journey's conversations creates the conversation
  in that Journey, even when another one is selected (CR207).
- **A tidier tree and a readable header.** A nested Journey's conversations sit under it (CR208), and
  "Working in ~/…" is legible on Daylight, Mist and Parchment (CR209).
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

The conversation list mixed Nautilus's conversations with dozens of Mirror conversations from other
runtimes, many empty, which could only be hidden. In the Tree view, New under any open group created
the conversation in the selected Journey instead; the conversations under a nested Journey drew their
line at the root's column; and on light themes the working folder's path was white on white.

## What Changed

- **Mirror history is turned off by one switch.** The app no longer asks the Core for a Journey's
  conversations from elsewhere, and the actions that used them (continue here, continue in Terminal,
  rename in Mirror) refuse before reaching the Core. A relaunch that had one of them open lands on
  the Journey. The code is kept, not deleted (CR206).
- **New carries its Journey.** The dialog reads "New Conversation in <Journey>", the suggested title
  is checked against that Journey's list, and after creating the app goes to it (CR207).
- **Indentation follows depth.** The conversation group indents by the tree's 16 pixels per level
  (CR208).
- **The folder line uses the theme's colours.** The path is no longer painted as a filled button on
  light themes, and the unavailable-folder amber has a darker light-theme form, measured at 4.8 to
  5.4:1 (CR209).

## What Does Not Change

The same Mirror core (`mirror-mind@0.40.0-next.1`) and the same Pi as v0.4.0-alpha.4. Nothing in a
home's database changes. Conversations created earlier from a Mirror conversation are ordinary
Nautilus conversations and keep working. Your stored preferences are kept.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **The agent can still recall a Mirror conversation when asked.** Mirror's recall skill runs inside
  the agent, not in the app; restricting it would be a Core decision.
- **The deletion dialog's pale list and title (CR205)** wait for a later version.
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

A button that belongs to a group must carry the group's identity. Reading "the selected one" was
right when there was a single list, and silently wrong once several could be open.

## Next Horizon

CR205, then re-examine the older open work (RS027).
