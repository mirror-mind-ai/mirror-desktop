# v0.4.0-alpha.2 — The Message Comes Back

**Date:** 2026-11-04

## Highlights

- **Cancel right after sending, and the message is yours again.** It returns to the composer with
  the notice "Message returned to the composer", ready to edit or send again (CR193).
- **No empty answer is left behind.** A cancelled turn whose answer held only the persona no longer
  stays in the conversation.
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

CR183 (v0.3.0-alpha.9) gave back a message whose turn was cancelled before any answer. CR188
(v0.3.0-alpha.12) then started naming the persona at the top of the answer as soon as the Mirror
delivers its context, which happens before Pi takes the message, often 20 to 40 seconds after Send.
The Navigator found in Nautilus that the message was lost again and the composer stayed empty.

## What Changed

- The persona marker no longer counts as an answer. A cancelled turn with no prose, no interrupted
  fragment and no work gives its message back, and its persona-only answer is dropped (CR193).

## What Does Not Change

The same Mirror core and the same Pi as v0.4.0-alpha.1. Nothing in a home's database, the app's data
folder or its preferences changes.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **The wait before Pi takes the message** is not shortened here; it is why this window is so easy
  to hit and may get its own change.
- **The journal records of these short cancels** still stop one step before `interrupted`; this did
  not cause the loss and is read separately.
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

Two changes that each passed their own check can still meet badly: one decided "unanswered" by an
empty answer, the other began writing into that answer earlier. The decision now lives in one named
function, tested with the persona present.

## Next Horizon

The Navigator's check in production, then RS027 and the launch.
