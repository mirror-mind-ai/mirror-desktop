# v0.3.0-alpha.13 — The Person at the Top Right

**Date:** 2026-11-04

## Highlights

- **A correction sent right after Send is taken.** It waits, pending, until the turn can receive
  it, instead of being refused.
- **The person is at the top right and on their turns.** A round button with the photo or initial
  shows the person's name, and their turns carry that name instead of "You".
- **Chapters is in the `⋯` menu**, and the reset dialog explains what a reset does, when it helps,
  and that Cancel is the safe default.
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

A correction sent in the first three seconds of a turn was refused, while one sent later was
taken (CR187). Pi was not the one refusing: the app asked before its own invocation could take the
correction. The header carried a Chapters glyph and no sign of whose Mirror this is; the person's
turns said "You" beside a hard-coded "N"; and the reset dialog did not say plainly that the agent
would forget the Conversation (CR190).

## What Changed

- **CR187.** While the invocation does not exist yet, has no Pi process yet, or has not sent the
  turn's prompt, the app answers "not yet" and writes nothing. The correction stays pending and is
  asked again every quarter second until it is taken, the turn ends (it is then recorded as
  unconsumed) or two minutes pass. A correction never reaches Pi ahead of the prompt it corrects,
  and a refused correction now keeps its reason.
- **CR190, the person.** The name is the one typed at setup ("What should your Mirror call you?"),
  or one written in Settings › You › Name, or the home's user read as a name (`alisson-vale` reads
  "Alisson Vale"). Its initial replaces the "N" in the transcript, the turn navigator and Settings.
- **CR190, the header.** At the top right: `⋯`, then the person button. Chapters is the first item
  of the `⋯` menu, separated from Reset agent context, and says in the item why it is unavailable
  when the Conversation has no chapters.
- **CR190, the reset dialog.** It says the agent starts with no memory of the Conversation, that the
  past generation stays readable but cannot be made active again, and that the Mirror's memories are
  untouched. It says when to reset, points to Compact now when the context is only long, has
  Settings' Close button, and makes Cancel the focused, emphasized action.
- **CR188, a remainder.** The model's `◇ persona` line no longer flashes in the live agent comments.

## What Does Not Change

The same Mirror core and the same Pi as v0.3.0-alpha.12. Nothing in a home's database changes; the
written name is kept with the app's preferences for that home. Mirror Desktop 0.2 is not offered.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **The Mirror's written name is not read from the core.** Its Desktop API does not expose the user
  identity; an extend-only core read can later replace the name taken from the home's user.
- **The 41 seconds between Send and Pi's prompt** that CR187 observed are not reduced here.
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

The refusal looked like Pi's and was the app's: reading Pi's source showed it queues a correction
at any point, which moved the repair to where the app asked too early. And a name the setup screen
already asked for was being thrown away; the Navigator's question found it before the field in
Settings became the only way to give it.
