# v0.2.0-alpha.9 — Reliable Agent Access

**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Highlights

- Lets a completed local response admit the next turn before secondary Mirror synchronization finishes.
- Keeps incomplete, failed, cancelled, stale, truncated, empty, or authority-mismatched evidence fail-closed.
- Replaces generic retry with explicit routes to repair Mirror synchronization, recover a preserved response, preserve an interrupted attempt, start a new Conversation, or reset agent context.
- Ensures recovery operations are model-free and never retry the provider implicitly.
- Prevents stale Pi leaves from masquerading as fresh completion evidence and preserves exact durable turn authority.
- Allows relevant files and historical material from another Journey to be read without changing the selected Journey's destination or mutation authority.
- Restores visible expansion controls and readable associated Conversation lists in Daylight, Mist, and Parchment themes.

## Where We Started

Mirror Desktop already preserved detailed execution, Conversation, journal, outbox, and Mirror synchronization evidence. That durability protected work, but several recoverable secondary failures could still remove practical access to the agent. A response could be durably projected locally while a later Mirror append or receipt step remained incomplete, yet the Conversation would continue blocking successors. Generic recovery language also obscured whether an action would repair synchronization, reuse preserved evidence, interrupt an attempt, or begin with fresh context.

The same broadness appeared in Journey authority. The selected Journey correctly governed run correlation, persistence, and implicit references, but the runtime prompt treated every piece of material naming another Journey as an authority violation. Transferred artifacts and legitimate cross-project evidence could therefore stop work even when they were explicitly selected as source material.

Continued alpha use also exposed a smaller but consequential navigation problem: the control that reveals a Journey's associated Conversations, and the list it opened, could lose contrast in light themes and make delivered Conversation capabilities appear absent.

## What Changed

Conversation availability now comes from one centralized decision that distinguishes live execution and unsafe durable evidence from recoverable synchronization debt. A completed projected turn may admit a successor when exact terminal evidence, persisted Conversation authority, chronology, and content all agree. The successor reloads the complete durable Conversation rather than relying on only the currently displayed Segment. Mirror append, receipt projection, and acknowledgement continue asynchronously and can be repaired without rerunning the model.

Fresh completion evidence is stricter. The current Pi branch must advance beyond its pre-invocation leaf, so an unsupported provider or failed run cannot borrow an older assistant response. Failed, cancelled, incomplete, empty, truncated, stale-generation, replaced, or chronologically invalid records remain blocking until an exact recovery route resolves them.

Recovery presentation now names the operation and consequence. Repairing Mirror synchronization retries only the pending append path. Recovering a preserved response projects exact durable evidence without invoking Pi. Preserving an attempt and continuing interrupts only an exact inactive successor-ineligible record while retaining terminal evidence. Starting a new Conversation and resetting agent context use their existing model-free authorities. Provider failures also terminate the in-memory finalization state instead of leaving the composer on **Finishing…**, and ordinary automatic synchronization remains quiet unless debt survives finalization.

Journey authority is now proportional to the operation. The selected Journey still owns the turn destination, Conversation persistence, run correlation, selected-Journey synthesis, and implicit references such as “this Journey.” Relevant material may retain provenance from another Journey and still be read. Loaded material, cwd, sticky state, recent state, or defaults cannot retarget the turn or grant administrative mutation or publication authority; those operations require explicit Navigator intent naming the exact target.

Light-theme styling now gives the Journey Conversation expansion control explicit semantic foreground, surface, expanded, hover, focus-visible, and disabled states. The associated focused Conversation list uses readable title, metadata, icon, selected-row, **New**, and more-action treatments across Daylight, Mist, and Parchment without changing hierarchy or interaction semantics.

## Conscious Exclusions

- Recovery does not retry a provider unless the Navigator explicitly sends a new request.
- Mirror synchronization is not discarded, downgraded, or treated as optional; it remains durable repairable work.
- Native Journey, generation, run, turn, cancellation, journal, outbox, and Conversation persistence guards are not relaxed.
- Cross-Journey material does not authorize a destination change, administrative Mirror mutation, or publication for a different Journey.
- No generic filesystem or prompt parser was introduced to classify Journey provenance.
- The light-theme correction does not redesign Journey cards, Conversation catalogs, accessibility labels, or theme palettes.
- Apple Developer ID signing, notarization, native arm64 packaging, stable-channel promotion, and app-store distribution remain outside this alpha release.

## What We Learned

Durability and availability are related but not identical. Preserving every stage of a turn is valuable only when each stage has the right operational consequence. Local completion should permit continued local work once its exact evidence is durable; remote synchronization should remain visible and repairable without becoming a second completion authority.

Recovery also becomes safer when actions describe one operation rather than promising a vague retry. Users can choose whether to repair delivery, project preserved evidence, retain an interrupted attempt, or start fresh, while the system avoids hidden model execution.

Journey selection follows the same proportional principle. It must be exact wherever destination or mutation is involved, but provenance is evidence, not authority. A transferred file can truthfully describe where it came from without deciding where the current turn belongs.

Finally, discoverability is part of capability. A technically available Conversation list is not meaningfully available when its expansion control and labels disappear into a light surface.

## Next Horizon

Observe the new availability and recovery boundaries through normal alpha use, especially interrupted provider paths, delayed Mirror synchronization, relaunch recovery, and transferred cross-Journey material. Continue collecting bounded usability adjustments under the refinement workbench while keeping release, persistence, and mutation authority explicit. Any future synchronization broadening should preserve the same separation between exact local completion, repairable remote delivery, and deliberate provider execution.
